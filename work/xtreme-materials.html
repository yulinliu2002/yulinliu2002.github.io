Materials Research · Jul 2022 – Jan 2023

Xtreme Materials

High-purity carbon-lithium composite synthesis via solvothermal methods in a controlled-atmosphere glove box.

01 — ContextCarbon-lithium composites are sensitive to almost everything.

Oxygen, moisture, handling, trace contamination — every variable degrades purity, and every step compounds the previous one. Producing them at the purity needed for materials research means doing the chemistry under nitrogen and tightening tolerances all the way down the chain.

02 — ConstraintsAir-sensitive precursors, micron-level fixtures.

Trace contamination ruins yield. Machining tolerances on the synthesis fixtures had to match the chemistry's purity targets — not by an order of magnitude, by ±10 µm. Process discipline is the experiment.

03 — What I BuiltSynthesis-to-handling chain inside the glove box.

  • Solvothermal synthesis with ±10 µm precision machining on the reaction fixtures.
  • Acid-washing, ultrasonication, and centrifugation for purification.
  • Nitrogen glove box environment for the full synthesis-to-handling sequence.

04 — OutcomeYield up, purity holding.

Yield increased 15%. Composite purity reached 93% — a working baseline for downstream characterisation work.
