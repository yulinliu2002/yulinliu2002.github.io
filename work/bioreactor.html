Biomechanics / Controls · Jan 2025 – Present

Cellxercise

A closed-loop bioreactor that replicates physiologically-relevant tensile conditions for tissue growth.

01 — ContextTissue grown in static culture doesn't behave like tissue in the body.

To study how cells respond under realistic mechanical conditions — tendons, ligaments, vasculature, anywhere the body cycles tension — the culture itself has to cycle. Cellxercise is the platform that does that, with the precision and sterility a wet-lab actually requires.

02 — ConstraintsSub-millimeter precision, 1 Hz forever, autoclavable end-to-end.

Closed-loop control had to hold a strain target across thousands of consecutive cycles without drift. Every part touching the culture — clamps, plates, fixtures — had to come out of an autoclave and go back in. And the actuator had to deliver real force (20 N) at biologically meaningful frequency, not a tabletop demo.

03 — What I BuiltMechanics, controls, and machining, end to end.

  • Bioreactor delivering 20 N at 10% cyclic strain, 1 Hz.
  • LabVIEW closed-loop control with a SMAC actuator and ATO load cell.
  • Sterilizable steel clamps and aluminum lift plates — designed in CAD, programmed in MasterCAM, machined on a HAAS CNC, and waterjetted on a WARDJet.

04 — OutcomeHolds the loop where it has to.

Under continuous operation, the system hits 0.01 mm positioning resolution and 5 µm measurement accuracy — fine enough to control strain at the cellular length-scale that matters for tissue mechanics.
