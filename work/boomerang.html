Research / Aerodynamics · Feb 2023 – Jun 2024

Boomerang Aerodynamics

An AIAA-published study on wing-tip deflection effects on flight stability, validated with UWB tracking.

01 — ContextWhat does bending the wing tips do to flight?

The Boomerang Research Lab studied joint-angle effects on flight stability — pairing simulation with field-measured data. The question is narrow but the methodology has to be careful: simulation only matters if you can measure something to verify it.

02 — ConstraintsOutdoor experiments are noisy.

Wind, throw variance, and tracking-rig calibration all show up in the data. Every trial has to either control for them or account for them. And every joint-angle configuration needs enough repetitions to separate the signal from the throw.

03 — What I BuiltPaper, models, instrumentation, field work.

  • Co-authored AIAA paper on wing-tip deflection.
  • MATLAB airflow models paired with UWB tracking algorithms for trajectory capture.
  • 15+ field trials across six joint angles, in low-wind conditions for measurement fidelity.

04 — OutcomeMeasurements validated the simulation.

Experimental data confirmed simulation predictions for joint-angle effects on stability — closing the loop between aero model and physical flight.
