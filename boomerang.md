<section class="project-detail">
  <h2>Challenge</h2>
  <p>
    Investigate how boomerang joint angles impact flight stability and aerodynamic performance.
  </p>

  <h2>Solution</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Conducted <strong>more than 60 field tests</strong> using <strong>UWB positioning</strong> at joint angles of <strong>60°, 90°, 105°, and 120°</strong>.</li>
    <li>Simulated airflow and torque behavior in <strong>MATLAB</strong> to evaluate lift and spin response.</li>
    <li>Analyzed angular velocity through video-based frame analysis and compared experimental data against simulation results.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Result</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Collected more than <strong>60 flight tests</strong>, with simulation and field data matching within <strong>10% error</strong>.</li>
    <li>Identified <strong>105°</strong> as the optimal joint angle, improving rotational stability by <strong>20%</strong>.</li>
  </ul>
