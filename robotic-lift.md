<section class="project-detail">
  <h2>Challenge</h2>
  <p>
    Design a compact robot to transport weighted blocks between platforms with a limited mechanical footprint.
  </p>

  <h2>Solution</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Developed a <strong>four-bar linkage lift</strong> with a <strong>1:4 torque-enhancing gear train</strong>.</li>
    <li>Designed a claw gripper and traction-based drivetrain modeled in <strong>SolidWorks</strong>.</li>
    <li>Prototyped the frame and structural parts using <strong>laser cutting</strong>.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Result</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Boosted payload capacity from <strong>3 kg to 4 kg</strong>, a <strong>30% increase</strong>.</li>
    <li>Achieved a lift cycle time of <strong>8 seconds</strong>, <strong>20% faster</strong> than baseline, over <strong>50 uninterrupted operations</strong>.</li>
  </ul>
