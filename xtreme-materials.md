<section class="project-detail">
  <h2>Challenge</h2>
  <p>
    Synthesize carbon-lithium composite materials under controlled environments for high-temperature and reactive settings.
  </p>

  <h2>Solution</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Performed <strong>solvothermal reactions</strong> in <strong>gloveboxes</strong> using carbon and lithium precursors.</li>
    <li>Applied <strong>ultrasonic treatment</strong>, <strong>centrifugation</strong>, and <strong>acid washing</strong> to improve sample purity.</li>
    <li>Established a repeatable lab workflow for handling reactive materials and purification steps.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Result</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Synthesized more than <strong>50 composite samples</strong> with <strong>greater than 99% purity</strong> and an average <strong>85% yield</strong>.</li>
    <li>Reduced total synthesis time by <strong>20%</strong> and improved electrical conductivity by <strong>15%</strong>.</li>
  </ul>
