<section class="project-detail">
  <h2>Challenge</h2>
  <p>
    Commercial bioreactor systems often lack the ability to apply both tension and compression in a controlled, incubator-compatible, and modular format, limiting research on musculoskeletal tissue regeneration.
  </p>

  <h2>Solution</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Designed a dual-mode actuator system using a <strong>SMAC LCA50</strong> capable of delivering cyclic loads up to <strong>20 N at 1 Hz</strong>.</li>
    <li>Implemented real-time feedback through a <strong>load cell</strong> and <strong>LabVIEW-based control</strong>.</li>
    <li>Developed <strong>modular clamps</strong> that are sterilizable and reusable.</li>
    <li>Ensured incubator compatibility and high force precision through iterative design refinement.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Result</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Achieved <strong>more than 90% strain recovery</strong> with <strong>less than 5% deviation</strong> from target across <strong>6,000 cycles</strong>.</li>
    <li>Maintained force precision within <strong>±0.2 N</strong>, reducing system drift by <strong>15%</strong> versus commercial systems.</li>
  </ul>
